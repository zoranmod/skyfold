**Skyfold 1.0.7 (public beta): sharper Apple Notes.**

### What's new
- **iPhone photos in notes at full size.** HEIC photos used to show as small previews (about 384 px). Skyfold now turns the original into a full-size JPEG with Windows' own HEIF decoder (the "HEIF Image Extensions" most PCs have). Without it, the preview stays as before.
- **Whole pen drawings.** Drawings used to show only a small square crop. Now the full drawing is used, also when it is tall and narrow or wide and low.
- **Notes.html updates again after upgrading from 1.0.5.** The searchable index was no longer refreshed for people who upgraded from 1.0.5 or earlier, so moved notes kept showing in their old folder. It is now taken back and updated (the old page goes to the Recycle Bin).
- After the update, only notes that still show small previews are exported again; the rest stay as they are.

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Download
`Skyfold-windows.zip` below; unzip anywhere and run `Skyfold.exe` (or replace your existing Skyfold folder; settings and sync state are kept). Check it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about a new, unsigned app: *More info → Run anyway*.
