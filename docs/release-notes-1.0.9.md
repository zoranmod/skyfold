**Skyfold 1.0.9 (public beta): a new look for your notes.**

### What's new
- **Notes.html redesigned to match the app.** The Skyfold name and logo at the top, your folders in a left column (with subfolders, and the number of notes in each), and notes grouped in cards per folder. Click a folder to see only its notes.
- **Works on a narrow screen too:** the folders are behind a *Folders* button.
- **Search** by title or folder name (Ctrl+K jumps to it, Esc clears it), light and dark theme, and collapsible folders that are remembered.
- Your existing Notes.html gets the new look once after the update, without downloading any notes again. Links to your exported notes and the protection of your own files are unchanged.

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Download
`Skyfold-windows.zip` below; unzip anywhere and run `Skyfold.exe` (or replace your existing Skyfold folder; settings and sync state are kept). Check it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about a new, unsigned app: *More info → Run anyway*.
