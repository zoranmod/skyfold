**Skyfold 1.0.13 (public beta): your Safari bookmarks in Edge, up to date by themselves.**

> On 1.0.10 or later? Skyfold offers this update by itself: Settings → About → *Check for updates* → *Update now*.

### What's new
- **The Skyfold – Safari Bookmarks extension for Edge.** With Safari bookmarks turned on in Skyfold (*Settings → Notes → Safari bookmarks*), the extension keeps a **Safari Bookmarks** folder in Edge the same as your Safari bookmarks: every 30 minutes, when Edge starts, and when you click *Update now*. No exporting, no importing, no duplicates. It gets the bookmarks from Skyfold on your PC and never goes online, and it only touches its own folder. Get it from Edge Add-ons (search "Skyfold") once it's listed there. Firefox and Chrome versions will follow.

### Fixed
- Safari bookmarks: Safari's Reading List was included; Safari's folders now have the names Safari shows (Favorites, Bookmarks Menu).

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Download
`Skyfold-windows.zip` below; unzip anywhere and run `Skyfold.exe` (or replace your existing Skyfold folder; settings and sync state are kept). Check it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`, or with `gh attestation verify Skyfold-windows.zip --repo zoranmod/skyfold`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about a new, unsigned app: *More info → Run anyway*.
