**Skyfold 1.0.11 (public beta): sharper and more complete images in your notes.**

> Already on 1.0.10? Skyfold offers this update by itself: Settings → About → *Check for updates* → *Update now*.

### What's new
- **Full-size photos in more cases**: also when the full image is kept on the attachment itself, when Apple lists a small thumbnail first, and when two notes share the same picture.
- **Previews are chosen by their real size in pixels**, not by file size.
- **Scanned documents** are shown as their full PDF (pages as images, the original linked), and **older drawings** with their full image; a drawing in a format browsers can't show (TIFF) is converted to PNG.
- **No more failed exports because of one image**: if a full-size image can't be downloaded, its preview is used and the note is still exported.
- iPhone (HEIC) photos keep a preview your browser can show until their full-size JPEG is made.
- Only notes that still show a small preview, a scan or an older drawing are exported again once after the update; all others stay as they are.

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Download
`Skyfold-windows.zip` below; unzip anywhere and run `Skyfold.exe` (or replace your existing Skyfold folder; settings and sync state are kept). Check it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about a new, unsigned app: *More info → Run anyway*.
