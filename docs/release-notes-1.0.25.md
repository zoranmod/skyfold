**Skyfold 1.0.25 (public beta): a colour for each service.**

> On 1.0.10 or later? Skyfold offers this update by itself: Settings → About → *Check for updates* → *Update now*.

### Changed
- **Each service has its own colour**, in the light, dark and OLED themes: Photos pink, iCloud Drive blue, Notes amber, Safari bookmarks violet. They sit on tinted tiles in the panel and colour the icons in Settings, the same as on the [website](https://zoranmod.github.io/skyfold/).
- **A soft glow** lights up a service's tile while the pointer is over its row (or the row has keyboard focus), and the Skyfold mark in the panel's header glows under the pointer.
- **The sync arrows next to "Syncing…" turn** while a pass runs.
- With animations turned off in Windows, the glow appears without fading and the arrows stay still.

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Download
Download `Skyfold-windows.zip` below, unzip it anywhere and run `Skyfold.exe`. To update by hand, quit Skyfold and replace `Skyfold.exe` and the `_internal` folder with the ones from the zip; keep the `data` folder (your settings).

To check the download, compare it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`, or run `gh attestation verify Skyfold-windows.zip --repo zoranmod/skyfold`.

Windows SmartScreen may warn about a new, unsigned app: choose *More info → Run anyway*.
