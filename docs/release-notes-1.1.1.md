**Skyfold 1.1.1 (public beta): the tray menu in the new look.**

- **The right-click menu of the tray icon** is now see-through like the tray panel and the notifications: the desktop shows through it, blurred, in your theme (light, dark or the nearly black OLED), with rounded edges and a soft shadow. With Windows' *Transparency effects* off it stays a plain menu in your theme's colours.

Everything new in 1.1 (the new look, Skyfold's own notifications and more): [Skyfold 1.1](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold/releases/tag/v1.1.0).

Download `Skyfold-windows.zip` below, unzip and run `Skyfold.exe`. Already using Skyfold? It updates itself: *What's new* → *Update now*. Your settings and files stay as they are.

To check the download, compare it with `Skyfold-windows.zip.sha256`, or run `gh attestation verify Skyfold-windows.zip --repo zoranmod/skyfold`.

**Na bosanskom:** meni na desni klik na Skyfoldovu ikonicu pored sata sada ima isti novi izgled kao panel i obavještenja: providan, sa zamućenom pozadinom u boji tvoje teme, zaobljenim ivicama i sjenom.
