**Skyfold 1.1 (public beta): a new look, everywhere.**

Skyfold is redesigned in the style of Windows 11, and gets its own notifications.

- **See-through, like Windows 11.** The tray panel, Settings, the Photos and Notes windows and every message: the desktop shows through, blurred (with Windows' *Transparency effects* on), with light layers on top, smooth rounded edges, new icons, and clear numbers. Light, Dark and a nearly black OLED theme.
- **Skyfold's own notifications** beside the tray, in your theme, with buttons that do the next thing (*Open*, *Show in folder*, *Review*, *What's new*), the new photos as small pictures, and a **bell** in the panel that keeps the ones that went away. Prefer Windows' own? *Settings → General → Notifications*.
- **The tray panel** shows progress as a ring with the speed and the time left, your services and iCloud storage at a glance, and recent activity with small photos.
- **Settings as cards**, with your Apple ID and your picture at the top (from your own card in Contacts; kept only on this PC).
- **First run in three short steps**, and a compact **deletion question** that shows the photos themselves.
- **Photos and Notes windows** in the new look; the Photos window can be switched off (*Settings → Photos*), then *Photos* opens the folder in File Explorer.

Download `Skyfold-windows.zip` below, unzip and run `Skyfold.exe`. Already using Skyfold? It updates itself: *What's new* → *Update now*. Your settings and files stay as they are.

**Na bosanskom:** Skyfold 1.1 ima potpuno novi izgled u stilu Windowsa 11: providan panel, Postavke, prozori Fotografije i Bilješke i sve poruke, sa zamućenom pozadinom, novim ikonicama i jasnim brojevima, u svijetloj, tamnoj i gotovo crnoj OLED temi. Skyfold sada ima i svoja obavještenja pored sata, sa sličicama novih slika, a zvonce u panelu čuva ona koja su nestala. Postavke su kartice sa tvojom slikom naloga na vrhu, prvo pokretanje ima tri kratka koraka, a pitanje za brisanje pokazuje same slike. Prozor Fotografije se može isključiti u Postavkama.
