# Privacy policy: Skyfold – Safari Bookmarks (browser extension)

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

**The extension collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **What it reads:** your Safari bookmarks, which the Skyfold app on the same PC hands to it through the browser's
  native messaging (a local connection between the browser and an app installed on the PC). The extension never
  connects to the internet, to iCloud, or to any server.
- **What it changes:** only the one bookmark folder it creates, "Safari Bookmarks". It never reads, changes or
  deletes any other bookmark.
- **What it stores:** in the browser's extension storage on your PC, the id of that folder, a fingerprint of the
  last bookmarks it received (to tell whether they changed), how many there were, and when it last updated. Nothing
  else, and nothing leaves your PC.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts.** The extension has no remote code.

The Skyfold app itself reads your Safari bookmarks from your own iCloud account, read-only, with the sign-in you
gave it; it has no server of its own. See the app's [privacy section](https://github.com/zoranmod/skyfold#privacy-and-security).
